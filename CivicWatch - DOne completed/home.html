THE SMALL PRINT, CLEARLY

Privacy and responsible use.

We believe policies should be understandable. Here are the essentials for this project demo.

CivicWatch stores demo account and report data locally in your browser. Information you submit is used to display reports and provide the product experience. Do not submit personal, sensitive, or emergency information in this demo.

CivicWatch is a demonstration platform and does not replace emergency services or an official municipal reporting channel. For emergencies or immediate hazards, contact local emergency services and the responsible authority directly.
